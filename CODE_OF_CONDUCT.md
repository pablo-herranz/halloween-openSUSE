# Code of conduct

halloween-openSUSE follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, welcome people of every
background and level of experience, give and take criticism gracefully, and
keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, discrimination and
unwelcome sexual attention are not tolerated.

It applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and
wherever someone represents the project. In openSUSE's own spaces (its
forums, mailing lists and chat), the
[openSUSE Code of Conduct](https://coc.opensuse.org/) applies
too.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Martin Pluskal, at
martin@pluskal.org. Reports are handled in confidence. Consequences follow
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a private correction
to a ban from the project.
