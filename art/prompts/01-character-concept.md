# 01 Character concept sheet

The chameleon's look, locked before any other art is made.

## Free drafts

Model `picsart-sana-sprint-v1`, 16:9, to explore the idea.

1. Concept sheet prompt (first version): came out frog-like, with no casque
   or turret eyes and a pale belly.
2. Forest scene, chameleon on mossy roots beside a lantern: right mood
   (birches, harvest moon, falling maple leaves), but a purple, realistic
   chameleon.

Lesson: name the chameleon anatomy explicitly and say the whole body is green.

## Paid run

| Model | Settings |
|---|---|
| `flux-2-pro` | 1K, 16:9, count 2 |
| `gemini-3.1-flash-image` (Nano Banana 2) | 1K, 16:9, count 2 |

Prompt (same for both):

> Character design sheet for an original cartoon chameleon mascot, cosy
> storybook illustration with soft gouache texture and warm candle-lit light.
> Unmistakably a chameleon, not a frog or lizard: rounded head casque crest,
> cone-shaped turret eyes with big friendly dark pupils, gripping two-toed
> feet, long prehensile tail rolled into a tight spiral. Its whole body is
> bright leaf-green with a soft yellow-green belly and subtle darker green
> speckles. It wears a floppy plum-purple witch hat with a burnt-orange band
> and a chunky hand-knitted scarf striped pumpkin-orange and rust red. Three
> views side by side on a plain warm cream background with soft ground
> shadows: front view sitting, three-quarter view hugging a carved
> jack-o'-lantern glowing with candlelight, side view on a branch with the
> tail curled. Same character and proportions in every view, clean readable
> silhouettes. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no signature.

## Result

Four candidates (only the chosen one is kept in the repository):

| | Model | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A | `flux-2-pro` | Frog-like front view, tail drawn on the belly |
| B | `flux-2-pro` | Cute, but frog-like faces |
| C | Nano Banana 2 | Most chameleon-like, adds a small lantern; has caption text (crop before reuse) |
| D | Nano Banana 2 | Clearly a chameleon (casque, turret eyes, spiral tail), consistent across views, no text |

## Chosen: D

Saved as `art/source/concept-sheet.png`
(1376x768). It is the reference image for every later character
generation.
