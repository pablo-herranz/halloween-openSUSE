# 10 Icons after the design review

The first design review (icons, colours and states, the brief, platform
rules) found the third round off-style and without seasonal touches, a few
older masters misread or looking like other projects' marks, and settings
icons that could not be told apart. These masters replace them.

Generated with the Picsart `gen-ai` command-line tool, count 2 each.

(`art/source/vectors/folder.svg`, rendered at 1024 px) as style reference,
image weight 60. Bold clear silhouette readable at 22 pixels, no outlines, no gradients, no texture, no drop shadow, straight front view. Centred with an even margin, plain white background. ## How they are used

- **Apps:** each master stands for the GNOME, Xfce and KDE apps that do the
  same job, under their current and older names (`APPS` in
  `tools/vectors.py`), YaST included.
- **Devices:** drives, removable media, servers, printers, phones, mice,
  keyboards, headphones, displays, batteries and computers (`DEVICES`).
- **Settings:** `settings-pumpkin` with a glyph or emblem carved into it,
  dark like an unlit jack-o'-lantern; the pumpkin's body is flattened to two
  oranges in the build, so the glyph reads at 16 px (`SETTINGS`).
- **Power and session:** `glyph-power` for shut down, `glyph-hibernate` for
  hibernate, `glyph-logout` for log out, `glyph-apps` for all applications
  (`SYMBOLIC`).
- **Colours:** `normalise()` moves the masters' purples to the hue of
  `plum_icon` in the palette, turns near-whites cream and lifts near-blacks
  off the night background.
