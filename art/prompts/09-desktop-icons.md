# 09 Desktop icons: devices and settings glyphs

The third icon round: the apps GNOME and Xfce ship by default, devices and
Xfce's settings dialogs. Generated with the Picsart `gen-ai` command-line
tool; image-to-image with the master folder as reference failed upstream at
the time, so these colour icons had no reference image. The design review
that followed found that they drifted from the set's style and carried no
seasonal touch, and all but three were made again with the reference: see
`10-design-review-icons.md`. Only the masters still in use are listed here.

## Colour icons

`recraftv4_1_utility_pro_vector`, no reference, count 2 each. Each prompt is
the subject, then "An application icon for a cosy autumn Halloween desktop icon theme in a flat vector style: very few large flat shapes, at most two tonal steps per colour, no stripes, no patterns, soft rounded corners, chunky friendly proportions, palette of pumpkin orange (#E8751A), apricot (#F5A05A), plum (#8952A0), dark plum (#65367B), cream (#F2E9E1), candle gold (#F0B429) and night purple (#231B2A). Bold clear silhouette readable at 22 pixels, no outlines, no gradients, no texture, no drop shadow, straight front view. Centred with an even margin, plain white background. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no signature."

| Master | Subject | Chosen |
|---|---|---|
| `device-phone` | A smartphone device icon: an upright plum-purple smartphone with rounded corners and a night-purple screen showing a small pumpkin-orange jack-o'-lantern. | 1 |
| `device-mouse` | A computer mouse device icon: a rounded plum-purple computer mouse seen from above with a pumpkin-orange scroll wheel and a short cream cable curling from its top. | 2 |
| `device-battery` | A battery device icon: an upright plum-purple battery with a cream terminal cap and a large pumpkin-orange lightning bolt on its front. | 2 |

## Emblem glyphs

`recraftv4_1_utility_vector`, no reference, count 2 each. Each prompt is "A
simple bold glyph icon of …" followed by:

> Solid black flat silhouette on a plain white background, with only a few small white negative-space cut-outs for detail. Single colour, no outlines, no gradients, no shading, very simple chunky shapes that stay readable at 16 pixels, centred and filling most of the square. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no signature.

| Master | Subject | Chosen |
|---|---|---|
| `glyph-keyboard` | a typing keyboard seen from the front: a wide rounded rectangle with two rows of small square holes and one long slot at the bottom (the first wording, "a computer keyboard with three rows of square keys and a long space bar", failed every time) | 2 |
| `glyph-mouse` | a computer mouse seen from above with a scroll wheel and a short curly cable | 2 |
| `glyph-panel` | a desktop screen with a solid bar along its bottom edge holding three small squares, like a taskbar panel | 2 |
| `glyph-window` | an application window with a thick title bar and three small round buttons on it | 1 |
| `glyph-bell` | a notification bell with a small bat perched on top of it | 1 |
| `glyph-battery` | an upright battery with a lightning bolt cut out of its middle | 2 |

They are used as described in `10-design-review-icons.md`.
